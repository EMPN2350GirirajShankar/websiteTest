## The challenge

Replenishment ran once a night against a 48-hour-old demand snapshot. Regional
managers were overriding the system by hand, and the overrides were not feeding
back into the forecast.

## What we built

- An agent that reads point-of-sale, weather, and promotion feeds continuously
- A reasoning loop that proposes order quantities with a written justification
- A review queue so planners approve, edit, or reject each proposal

## Results

| Measure | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stockout rate | 8.1% | 5.3% |
| Manual overrides per week | 1,200 | 190 |
| Planner hours per week | 60 | 22 |

Planners now spend their time on exceptions rather than on data entry.

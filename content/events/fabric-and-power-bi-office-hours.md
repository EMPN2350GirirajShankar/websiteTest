## What to expect

An informal, question-driven hour with the engineers who run large Fabric and
Power BI estates every day. No slides unless a question needs one.

## Topics we usually cover

- Capacity sizing and cost control
- Semantic model design for large fact tables
- Report performance triage
- Deployment pipelines and governance

## Who should attend

Data platform owners, BI leads, and analytics engineers who already run
workloads on Microsoft Fabric or Power BI.

## About the role

You will design and ship production data platforms on Microsoft Fabric,
Databricks, and Azure for enterprise clients, owning your work from
architecture through to release.

## What you will do

- Model and build lakehouse pipelines that stay correct under real load
- Tune Spark and SQL workloads against cost and latency targets
- Set the data quality and observability standards a team can follow
- Mentor engineers and take part in design reviews

## What we are looking for

- 5+ years building production data pipelines
- Strong SQL and Python; Spark experience preferred
- Hands-on Azure experience; Fabric or Databricks a plus
- Clear written communication — we work with clients directly
